## Top Mission Planning Software Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Defense, Aerospace, Space Mission Design, C2 Planning, Simulation & Operational Mission Management*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Mission Planning**. These systems support planning, simulation, analysis, and execution of military, aerospace, and space missions—covering routes, sensors, orbits, force structures, and command-and-control workflows.



**Examples** include AFSIM Cloud, AGI STK, Ansys STK, Windward Mission Planner, ForeFlight Military, Palantir Mission Manager, Systematic SitaWare, C2 Mission Manager, BlackSky Spectra, and Orbit Logic (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full operational military mission-planning and C2 suites are almost entirely commercial or government-program specific. Strong open options exist mainly in **space mission analysis** (**NASA GMAT**, **Orekit**, Open Space Toolkit). Flight-planning and research simulation tools fill narrower niches. This section is realistic about the defense C2 gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[AFSIM (Advanced Framework for Simulation, Integration, and Modeling)](https://www.afsim.com/)**  

  U.S. Air Force–originated simulation framework widely used for mission-level analysis, wargaming, and operational assessment (cloud and enterprise deployments).



- **[AGI STK / Ansys STK](https://www.ansys.com/)**  

  Industry-standard Systems Tool Kit for physics-based modeling, simulation, and visualization of land, sea, air, and space missions (sensors, orbits, communications, coverage).



- **[Windward Mission Planner](https://www.windwardsoftware.com/)**  

  Maritime and mission-oriented planning tools used in shipping and related operational contexts (route and voyage planning heritage).



- **[ForeFlight Military](https://www.foreflight.com/)**  

  Military-oriented flight planning, briefing, and electronic flight bag capabilities built on the ForeFlight aviation platform.



- **[Palantir Mission Manager / Gotham-related mission tools](https://www.palantir.com/)**  

  Mission management and operational planning capabilities within Palantir’s defense and intelligence platforms.



- **[Systematic SitaWare](https://systematic.com/)**  

  C2 and mission-planning suite used by defense forces for headquarters and operational command-and-control workflows.



- **[C2 Mission Manager solutions](https://www.example.com/)**  

  Category of command-and-control mission management products offered by various defense software providers for planning and execution tracking.



- **[BlackSky Spectra](https://www.blacksky.com/)**  

  Geospatial intelligence and mission-support platform combining satellite imagery, analytics, and operational awareness.



- **[Orbit Logic](https://www.orbitlogic.com/)**  

  Mission planning and scheduling software specialized in spacecraft, collection planning, and space-system operations.



- **[Additional STK / Ansys mission analysis offerings](https://www.ansys.com/)**  

  Extended modules and cloud options around the STK ecosystem for multi-domain mission engineering.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[NASA GMAT (General Mission Analysis Tool)](https://github.com/nasa/GMAT)**  

  Leading open-source, multi-mission space mission design, optimization, and navigation system supporting LEO to deep-space regimes.



- **[Orekit](https://gitlab.orekit.org/orekit/orekit)**  

  Low-level space dynamics library widely used for orbit propagation, attitude, and flight dynamics—foundation for many open and commercial space tools.



- **[Open Space Toolkit – Astrodynamics](https://github.com/open-space-collective/open-space-toolkit-astrodynamics)**  

  Open libraries for flight profiles, orbits, attitude, and access analysis aimed at modern space mission applications.



- **[satkit and Orekit-based Python mission tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Higher-level Python packages built on Orekit for satellite mission analysis and design.



- **[Open flight planning and EFB research projects](https://github.com/)**  

  Community flight-planning and aviation tools that can support research or non-operational planning use cases.



- **[Orbital mechanics and visualization open libraries](https://github.com/)**  

  Libraries for two-body propagation, ground-track visualization, and basic coverage analysis.



- **[Simulation and wargaming open frameworks](https://github.com/)**  

  Research-oriented open simulation environments sometimes used for conceptual mission studies (not operational C2).



- **[Geospatial open stacks for mission support](https://github.com/)**  

  Open GIS and imagery tools (QGIS, OpenLayers, etc.) used to build custom situational-awareness layers.



- **[Scheduling and optimization open solvers](https://github.com/)**  

  General optimization libraries applied to collection planning, resource allocation, and routing problems.



- **[Secure collaboration open platforms for planning cells](https://github.com/)**  

  Self-hosted collaboration tools adapted for planning documents and briefings under appropriate security controls.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using **NASA GMAT** and **Orekit** as the core of open space mission design and analysis pipelines.

- Combining open orbital libraries with commercial imagery or C2 systems for hybrid workflows.

- Building research or training mission scenarios with open simulation and geospatial tools.

- Accepting that operational military mission planning, certified flight planning for defense aviation, and full C2 suites still require commercial or government-authorized platforms (STK, AFSIM, SitaWare, Palantir, ForeFlight Military, Orbit Logic, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on space flight dynamics, research, education, and non-operational analysis.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Design space trajectories in GMAT/Orekit → analyze coverage and access with open toolkits → visualize in open GIS → integrate results into authorized operational systems as required. Suitable for research, academia, and commercial space teams. Defense operational users must follow applicable security, export-control, and accreditation rules and typically rely on approved commercial or government systems.
